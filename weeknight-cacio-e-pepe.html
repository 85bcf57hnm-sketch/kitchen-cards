Weeknight Cacio e Pepe

MainItalian20 minServes 2

Ingredients

  • 8 oz tonnarelli or spaghetti
  • 1 1/2 cups finely grated Pecorino Romano
  • 2 tsp coarsely ground black pepper
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter

Method

  1. Cook pasta in a wide pan with less water than usual, so the starchy water stays concentrated.
  2. Toast pepper in a dry skillet until fragrant, then add butter and a ladle of pasta water.
  3. Mix pecorino with a few tablespoons of warm pasta water into a smooth paste.
  4. Move pasta to the skillet, toss off the heat, and work in the cheese paste, adding water until glossy.
Notes
Sample recipe — edit or delete anytime. Keep the pan off the heat when adding cheese or it will clump.
