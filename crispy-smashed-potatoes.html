Crispy Smashed Potatoes

SideAmerican55 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb baby Yukon Gold potatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp flaky salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 cloves garlic, grated
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 lemon, zested

Method

  1. Boil potatoes in well-salted water until a knife slides in easily, about 20 minutes. Drain and let steam dry 5 minutes.
  2. Set the oven to air fry at 425°F. Oil a sheet pan.
  3. Smash each potato flat with the bottom of a glass. Brush tops with oil and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Air fry 20–25 minutes until deeply browned and crisp at the edges.
  5. Toss with garlic, parsley and lemon zest while hot.
